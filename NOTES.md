# Research notes — the news of Tuesday 17 November 2026

Every story on the front page happened, or was first reported, on
17 November 2026. Each was verified against dated reporting before it was
drawn; sources and the dates they carry are listed per story.

## The six stories chosen

1. **Ukraine cripples Crimea Bridge** — Ukraine's missile-and-drone strike
   partly collapsed the Kerch (Crimea) Bridge; President Zelensky hailed an
   "unprecedented" attack and Russia suspended traffic across it.
   Sources: BBC News live feed, 17 Nov 2026 21:24 ("Putin's Crimean Bridge
   partly collapses as Zelensky hails 'unprecedented' attack by Ukraine");
   Al Jazeera, 17 Nov 2026 (Ukrainian attack targets Kerch Bridge);
   ASAS Press world digest, published 17 Nov 2026.

2. **Tankers hit mines in Hormuz** — another tanker caught fire after
   striking a naval mine in the Strait of Hormuz (Iran said it was the fourth
   in 24 hours) as the US–Iran war widened; Bahrain also intercepted Iranian
   drones the same day.
   Sources: Al Jazeera, 17 Nov 2026 (Bahrain thwarts drone attack; Iran warns
   vessels in the strait); Daily Baku world feed, 17 Nov 2026 ("Two more
   ships hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz"); morning headlines roundup of
   17 Nov 2026 (fourth tanker hit by mine; IRGC seizes US drone).

3. **Chip rout rocks global markets** — the AI/chip selloff deepened as the
   US tightened AI-chip export curbs, and Nvidia faced its first earnings
   since CEO Jensen Huang's shock resignation the week before.
   Sources: Reuters, 17 Nov 2026 ("Chip wreck" markets commentary; chip
   shares tumbled, Nvidia results due after Tuesday's bell) and Reuters
   Breakingviews, 17 Nov 2026 ("Nine questions for Nvidia's stand-in CEO").

4. **US loses measles-free status** — the United States lost its measles
   elimination status after 26 years, with more than 5,400 cases and 10
   deaths reported this year — a 40-year high.
   Sources: BBC News live feed, 17 Nov 2026 21:04 ("US loses measles
   elimination status after 26 years amid record case numbers"); Daily Baku,
   17 Nov 2026 16:32 ("US measles cases hit 40-year high").

5. **Israel votes out Netanyahu ally** — the Knesset elected Matan Kahana of
   Gadi Eisenkot's bloc as speaker by 66–52, replacing Netanyahu ally Amir
   Ohana — a first step toward rolling back Netanyahu's agenda after the
   deadlocked October election.
   Source: Reuters, 17 Nov 2026 ("Eisenkot-led bloc replaces Netanyahu ally
   as Knesset speaker").

6. **APEC opens in Shenzhen** — leaders gathered in Shenzhen for the APEC
   summit week; Quad leaders met on 17 November and US President Trump was
   in Asia ahead of Thursday 19 November's first-ever US–China–Russia
   trilateral summit with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin.
   Sources: News of Bahrain world page, dated 17 Nov 2026 ("Quad leaders
   reaffirm Indo-Pacific commitment"); BBC News live feed, 17 Nov 2026 20:25
   ("Trump in Asia ahead of talks with Russian and Chinese presidents").

## Stories checked and deliberately left off

- **India's one-wicket U19 Asia Cup final win over Pakistan** — widely
  re-reported on 15–17 Nov, but the final itself was played on Saturday
  14 November (Cricbuzz match report dated 14 Nov 2026), so it is not
  17 November's news.
- **Xi–Trump–Putin trilateral summit as a standalone story** — the summit
  itself is on 19 November and was announced on 13–14 November (China
  confirmed on the 16th), so it appears only as the forward context inside
  the APEC panel, whose 17 November events are the Quad meeting and the
  leaders' arrival in Shenzhen.
- **COP31 draft deal** — the draft text was published on Monday 16 November,
  so it belongs to the previous day's contest.
